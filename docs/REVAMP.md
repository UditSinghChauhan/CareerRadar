# Landing revamp: references and the techniques borrowed

Studied on 2026-11-01 with headless Chromium at 1440 × 900, four frames per site, scrolled 1,100px apart. Techniques only; no look, asset, copy or code was taken from any of them.

## Apple (apple.com)
Each product gets a full screen with a two-word headline and the object large beneath it, and the page alternates scenes rather than stacking sections. **Borrowed:** one sentence per screen and the product as the largest thing on it; the pinned chapter's beats are paced like Apple's product reveals, scrubbed by scroll rather than played on a timer.

## Lightspark (lightspark.com)
Product cards float in a field of blurred, softly lit colour, and depth comes from light and focus, not from borders. **Borrowed:** light as atmosphere with one job: the radial light behind the radar and a soft glow on what is active. Not borrowed: the colourful blurred backdrops, which would compete with the single lime signal.

## Axiom (axiom.co)
A dark data product whose hero is its own query editor, framed edge to edge, with dense, precise type and small numerals. **Borrowed:** the real product as the hero object and close-ups of real UI at a readable size; tabular numerals for every count.

## Neatlogs (neatlogs.com)
A dark developer-tool page that pins one product panel and steps a small vertical indicator beside short copy as the panel's state changes. **Borrowed:** the pinned stage with a step indicator ("01 / 04" in lime), copy kept short and centred, and the panel changing state underneath.

## Shaders (shaders.com)
Luminous effects sit on a near-black stage; the light reads as coming from inside the object. **Borrowed (as inspiration for the radar's light only):** light that seems to come from the instrument, the found roles emitting a glow instead of being outlined.

## Rauno Freiberg (rauno.me)
Huge type cropped by its frame, one bold shape per card, and micro-interactions you only notice when you look. **Borrowed:** the micro-detail bar: hover states that light rather than move, numerals that count, the "Live" dot, and motion that finishes before you think about it.

## Stripe Press (press.stripe.com)
The page is WebGL and did not render in headless Chromium here (the screenshot timed out after 30s on a blank stage). What follows is from earlier knowledge of the site, not from this session: restraint, a single typeface set with confidence, and objects presented one at a time. **Borrowed:** typographic confidence: one family, very large display sizes, sentence case, nothing tracked out.

## Vibrant Design (vibrant.design/sections/bento, /sections/how-it-works)
Galleries of bento and step sections from shipped sites. The strong bento grids mix two large tiles with several small ones and put a working fragment of the product in each; the weak ones repeat an icon, a heading and a line of text. **Borrowed:** a 12-column bento with two large and four small tiles, each a looping micro-demo of a real feature rather than an icon card; steps shown as states of one object rather than as numbered boxes.

## What this adds up to
A dark, continuous stage; one object (the radar) that changes state from chapter to chapter; type at 96px; light used sparingly and only on what matters; the real product shown large, then in close-up.
